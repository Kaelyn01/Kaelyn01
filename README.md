## About Me

I am a **master's student at Fudan University**, working with the **Data Intelligence Lab**, and a **Machine Learning Engineer Intern at ByteDance**. My research focuses on multimodal post-training, reinforcement learning, and reliable agent systems, connecting model training with evaluation and practical applications.

## Research & Open Source

- **Multimodal post-training** — Fine-grained visual understanding, on-policy distillation, and selective supervision. [Models and datasets](https://huggingface.co/Kaelyn01)
- **CDBench** — A multimodal change-detection dataset spanning remote sensing, industrial inspection, and product images. [Dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MM-CD/CDBench)
- **Agent systems** — Tool use, memory, multi-agent orchestration, and learning from interaction experience.
- **Multi-City Inventory–Routing Optimization** — An independent optimization case study with exact search and reproducible results. [Code](https://github.com/Kaelyn01/Meituan-Logistics-Optimization)
